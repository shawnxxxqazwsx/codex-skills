# Production measurement and smoothness acceptance

Use before every new Pigora EQ fitting/module/product validation PASS, for all
native identities, complete modules and point adapters. Root `AGENTS.md`,
"Pigora measured-response and smoothness acceptance", owns contract
`pigora-eq-measurement-acceptance-20261001-v1`. The reviewed owner 0.5 dB fallback
prospectively overrides older unconditional product/inference 0.2 dB-only
wording; it does not alter targets, provenance or historical judgments.
Owner addendum `pigora-eq-compact-extrema-smoothness-20261006-v2` additionally
requires the exact extrema matrix and a frozen quantitative measured
[smoothness profile](smoothness-gate.md).
Use [smoothness-execution.md](smoothness-execution.md) for the explicit plan,
admitted/excluded scope, rate bases, exact states/contexts and preflight. V2
clarifies execution; no old certificate becomes a new PASS by relabeling it.

## One certificate, three required judgments

Bind actual production processed-audio measurements to an independently frozen
target. Compute every required finite identity/state/rate/frequency row's signed
and absolute error, with exact row membership and effective-control coverage.
Primary acceptance is inclusive `abs(measured_db - target_db) <= 0.2 dB` for
every required rate, independently. Report per-rate and global worst rows;
mean/RMS, UI plots, fitting helpers and expected values regenerated by production
formulas cannot establish this gate.

Preserve raw impulse/sweep/stepped-sine or equivalent captures and extraction
parameters. Bind source/target/grid/capture hashes, production binary/verifier,
native controls, sample rate, layout/channel path, block size, settled conditions,
calibration/reference, resolution, noise/truncation masks and uncertainty.
Calibration and uncertainty must support the selected tier. Missing, duplicate,
nonfinite, invalid or unresolved rows cannot pass. Retain explicit genuinely
unsupported or above-Nyquist reasons under the frozen scope; no failing region
may disappear. No post-hoc gain/frequency alignment is permitted to obtain PASS.
Hash-compatible complete audio receipts may be reused without recapture.

`certificate_status=PASS` requires `magnitude_status=PASS`,
`compact_matrix_status=PASS` and `smoothness_status=PASS`, with complete
coverage and provenance. The compact matrix is additional to the unchanged
original fitting gates: missing maximum-gain/Q endpoint combinations cannot
receive matrix PASS merely because all frozen target rows pass. Matrix states
without an authorized target remain separate engineering shape probes; do not
create or extrapolate external targets to fill them. It supplements
public/inference numeric evaluation and never replaces realtime, safety,
integration, required hosted checks or deployment authority. Its guarantee is
the declared finite plan, not infinitely many continuous positions.

## Prioritize reviewed fallback when primary remains unattainable

After repeated bounded, defensible corrections fail 0.2 dB, retain attempts,
candidates and exact worst/failed rows. Localize the source/model/runtime/
measurement layer and provide sensitivity or residual-floor evidence explaining
why further 0.2 dB attempts lack a defensible hypothesis in scope. No universal
proof of impossibility or endless retry loop is required; one poor candidate,
inconvenience or elapsed time alone is insufficient. Target/control/topology
ambiguity or unresolved measurement error cannot be covered by wider tolerance.

A non-author reviewer binds unchanged target/grid hash, affected identities/
rates/states/rows, failed attempts and reasons, exact `gate_db=0.5` and a separate
decision ID before the new fallback judgment. Existing stable-candidate review
may cover these subjects together; this does not require another reviewer chain
or owner-permission turn. A retained candidate may receive a new fallback
decision while its original 0.2 dB FAIL remains intact.

Prioritize passing every reviewed fallback row at inclusive `abs(error) <= 0.5
dB`, with other rows retaining 0.2 dB. Any row above its gate, missing row or
unresolved shape finding blocks the certificate. No third threshold, target
rewrite, omitted controls, averaging or holdout retuning is authorized. Label
any certificate using fallback `measurement-fallback-0.5`, not `primary-0.2`;
report primary/fallback counts and results separately.

Public rows use training-contract §6's reviewed public `fallback-0.5` decision
and re-versioned domain, without changing source values. Inference/engineering
rows use `owner-measurement-fallback-0.5` in a separate measured-product ledger;
they never enter public-fit or upgrade evidence status. The old named
`sample-rate-engineering-0.5` exception still requires a complete genuine 48 kHz
0.2 dB baseline. A 48 kHz owner measurement fallback cannot unlock it; required
high rates instead pass primary measurement or their own reviewed owner
measurement fallback. Every rate is judged independently.

## Objectively smooth at both magnitude tiers

Follow [curve-coverage.md](curve-coverage.md#compact-production-control-shape-gate)
for native acceptance units, mandatory maximum boost/cut × frequency × Q/shape
states and control tracks. Every required unique matrix/track state needs actual
processed-audio coverage or a complete matching reused capture. Follow
[smoothness-gate.md](smoothness-gate.md) for spacing-normalized frequency/control
derivatives, chord/prominence/join/feature-motion metrics, numeric profiles,
uncertainty propagation and hard-limit/refinement-trigger verdicts.
Before judgment, freeze finite
frequency/control grids, local refinement rule, guard version and quantitative,
resolution-aware limits justified by source/native-law expectations and
measurement resolution. Cover supported plateaus, skirts, cutoff/tails, joins,
narrow features, extremes, interpolation knots and the declared near-Nyquist
region. Reuse the same measured curves.

Judge unexpected magnitude steps, log-frequency slope/curvature, extra extrema/
shoulders and exterior offsets, plus whole-response, peak/transition location
and bandwidth changes between neighboring continuous-control states. Merely
looking smooth or counting derivative signs is insufficient. Preserve legitimate
resonance, asymmetry, steep cutoffs and native discrete switching; no generic
single-peak, symmetry or linear-interpolation rule. Record discrete switch
behavior separately from unexplained continuous interpolation jumps.

Refine flagged intervals with actual measurements. A diagnostic trigger may
close only within frozen limits or by independent source evidence / an already
authorized frozen modeling/native-law rule permitting the exact feature; a
candidate implementation explaining its own defect is not closure evidence.
Hard violations cannot be waived. Unresolved intervals
and exhausted refinement with pending findings cannot PASS. Do not smooth plotted
or exported data, weaken shape limits or waive findings because magnitude passed
0.5 dB. Validate changed guards with retained known-bad/corrected response pairs
as prescribed by curve-coverage; disclose synthetic-only sensitivity evidence.

## Receipt and legacy tooling

Record contract/certificate ID, author/reviewer, complete target/source/grid and
measurement-plan/capture/export hashes, binary/verifier/guard, calibration and
uncertainty, required/measured/missing/invalid rows and effective-state counts,
per-rate gates/worst rows/decision IDs, attempts and fallback review, smoothness
profile/hash, metrics/limits/uncertainty and threshold roles,
required/measured/missing extrema combinations and exact endpoint readback,
failed/flagged/refined/resolved states and evidence, separate
magnitude/compact-matrix/smoothness/certificate verdicts, reused receipts and
explicit nonclaims.
Distinguish confirmed nonfinite DSP/hard violations (FAIL) from missing/invalid
acquisition or unresolved uncertainty (PENDING). A predeclared legitimately
absent feature is NOT_APPLICABLE only for that metric, retaining whole-response
checks. Aggregate required constituents as FAIL > PENDING > PASS; retain
omission counts even when the certificate FAILs. Empty required coverage is
PENDING. Bind both exact-file `plan_sha256` and canonical `coverage_sha256`.

The owning production verifier computes per-row errors and explicitly adjudicates
the versioned certificate, with independent review of raw evidence and coverage.
`validate_public_fit.py` remains a public numeric validator only;
`validate_inference_fit.py` retains its strict legacy schema and cannot adjudicate
a 48 kHz 0.5 dB owner measurement fallback. Preserve its FAIL and use a separate
versioned measured-product export/receipt. Never edit an old report into PASS or
accept only a producer-written PASS summary.
