# Evidence-based lessons from external AI crypto work

Sources fetched on2026-09-26. Reverify changed product behavior when implementing it. No outside case supplies a guaranteed return for this experiment.

- [Freqtrade lookahead analysis](https://docs.freqtrade.io/en/latest/lookahead-analysis/) describes how full-series indicators can leak future candles. Test prefix invariance for signals AND forecast training sets; a passing test only supports the covered paths. A backtest cannot substitute for dry-run fill validation.
- [CryptoBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00417), a research benchmark, distinguishes information retrieval from prediction. Track source correctness separately from forecast calibration and trading economics. Its task scores are not win rates or PnL. If future forecasts use probabilities, save those before outcomes and report Brier/log-loss/calibration separately from net return; do not invent probabilities to fill fields.
- [CryptoTrade](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.09546v2) reports different performance across assets and regimes. Include losing regimes and buy-and-hold/cash baselines, disclose costs and all tried variants, and require an ablation or unseen comparison before asserting that an LLM reflection/news module improved returns. A paper's backtest is not verified live performance.
- [Hummingbot repository](https://github.com/hummingbot/hummingbot) and [FreqAI documentation](https://github.com/freqtrade/freqtrade/blob/develop/docs/freqai.md) provide engineering workflows, not profit guarantees. Paper connectors and rolling training are useful patterns; do not install/run an external bot merely because it has a promising README. Verify actual simulated fee/funding/fill coverage before adopting a connector.

Public competition leaderboards require organizer identity, fixed period, starting equity, complete trades and cost/accounting provenance. In the initial study, alpha-arena.io could not be established as nof1's official primary site; its profits/claims were excluded from evidence. A fetched similarly named site is not enough to establish identity. Apply web-research's identity rule rather than adding its claims to a forecast.

Review each forecast mismatch at the next safe checkpoint as requested; do not defer detecting problems until a weekly report. Consolidate repeated cause classes and change skills only when a durable instruction gap is supported. Preserve the old forecast, strategy and result. Routine market variance is a valid unresolved diagnosis, not an excuse to retrofit a profitable story.
