# Freeze expectation, reconcile outcome, learn without hindsight

For every investment decision (entry/add/reduce/hold/exit), record a new immutable decision id before its effective time. State expected NET PnL in USDT over a fixed horizon, reference equity/exposure, all costs, source/model/version, and a scenario interval. Supply probabilities only with a calibrated basis; a plausible range is not a statistically calibrated confidence interval. Preserve prior forecasts when updating a hold decision and distinguish incremental from full-trade PnL to avoid double counting. A no-new-position cycle records expected trading PnL0 for that cycle, not a forecast of the market's return or of existing positions.

Compare like with like: realized closed-trade net PnL to an entry/exit forecast, or marked liquidation equity at the frozen horizon to a hold forecast. Late closes, open positions and partial exits must be labeled; they do not count as realized final outcomes. Include fees, funding, slippage and measured model/runtime costs, or explicitly list excluded costs. Time differences and cost exclusions cannot be hidden in a profit number.

Required forecast fields: decision_id, frozen_at_utc, effective_after_utc, evaluation_at_utc, evaluation_rule (fixed_horizon or close_or_deadline), mode, action, baseline_equity_usdt, expected_net_pnl_usdt, scenario_low_usdt, scenario_high_usdt, mismatch_tolerance_usdt, forecast_basis, cost_assumptions, input_hashes, reviewer_verdict. Tolerance is fixed before action; default is0.01 USDT accounting precision, not a license to enlarge tolerance after observing error. Every finalized numerical error beyond tolerance, including a better-than-expected result, triggers retrospective; range breach and early risk-limit breach are additional triggers. Numeric forecasts without a defensible basis block new entry.

Use `scripts/forecast_record.py freeze input.json decision-directory` before action and `settle outcome.json decision-directory` after reconciliation. Files are created exclusively, never overwritten. It checks structure/arithmetic and raises retrospective_required; it does not verify source truth or execute retrospective by itself. Primary invokes the installed skill-retrospective immediately at the next safe checkpoint if required.

Outcome fields: decision_id, observed_at_utc, measurement_basis (realized_net_pnl or horizon_liquidation_pnl), actual_net_pnl_usdt, evidence_paths, costs_reconciled, final, early_risk_breach. Record whether any excluded/unmeasured costs remain in evidence. Outcomes cannot precede decision effectiveness. A forecast frozen with close_or_deadline permits normal early realized closure without calling it a risk breach; fixed_horizon requires the horizon valuation unless an actual risk breach interrupted the experiment. Never rewrite the original forecast to accommodate an exit. Research-only mode permits NO_NEW_POSITION only.

Retrospective artifact: link frozen forecast, outcome and reviewer; quantify signed/absolute error and range breach; classify data failure, implementation/execution error, stale assumptions, uncalibrated forecast, ordinary market variance or unresolved cause. Diagnose before modifying the narrow personal skill. A single unexpected loss or gain does not establish a new market law. If no durable instruction gap is supported, record 'no skill change justified'; this still satisfies the retrospective requirement. When a reusable gap is supported, version the rule, preserve old history, validate the skill, get Sol/high independent review of changed decision behavior and test out of sample before claiming improvement. Do not optimize rules to recoup losses or silently relax risk limits.
