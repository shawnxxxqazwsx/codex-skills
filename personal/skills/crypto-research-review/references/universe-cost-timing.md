# Universe, transaction costs and timing evidence

A one-symbol study does not answer a multi-asset strategy question. Predeclare a justified candidate universe before comparative results, distinguish historical point-in-time membership from present-day survivors, and disclose the selection limitation. Apply one fixed strategy/cost convention first; preserve losing candidates. Independent 1000-unit accounts are comparisons, not a combined 1000-unit portfolio. A shared portfolio needs explicit allocation, overlap, correlated risk and total margin constraints.

Reconcile each trade: raw-price gross PnL minus slippage minus commissions plus net funding equals net PnL. Slippage embedded in fills is attribution, not a second deduction. Report absolute costs and an explicit ratio denominator; cost/net-loss ratios can exceed100% and do not mean eliminating costs would preserve the same fills. A fee-only sensitivity keeps quantities/times/fills fixed and must not be called a maker execution backtest. Maker assumptions require queue/fill/timeout/adverse-selection handling; current personal rates and historical instrument rules remain separately verifiable inputs.

For timing requests provide UTC and user's local-time entry/exit, direction, execution resolution, costs, forecast and outcome for every trade. Distinguish post-hoc highest-profit trades, exploratory hour-of-day aggregates, and a prospective timing rule. OHLC data cannot identify exact best minute, intrabar sequence or attainable trough-to-peak profits. Do not imply synthetic hour-end timestamps are observed executions. Freeze prospective timing/cost filters before unseen validation; choosing the best asset/hour on an observed sample is selection, not validation.
